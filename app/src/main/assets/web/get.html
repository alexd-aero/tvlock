Installing it

  1. Open the download (tap the notification, or Downloads).
  2. If your phone asks, let your browser install apps: Settings → turn on Allow from this source → go back → Install.
  3. If Google Play Protect warns you (“Unsafe app blocked” or “Scan app?”), tap More details → Install anyway (or Install without scanning).
Why does Play Protect warn about it? TV Lock isn't on the Play Store, so Play Protect hasn't seen it before and warns about any app installed from a file. It's the same app that runs on your TV. It only talks to your TV, on your home network.
